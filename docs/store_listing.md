## 🚀 Powerful Features That Drive Results

### ⏱️ **Smart Pomodoro Timer**
Never lose track of time again. Our visual timer badge shows your remaining focus time right in your toolbar.

- Fully customizable work/break intervals (default: 25min work, 5min break)
- Auto-start next session to maintain momentum
- Smart notifications when sessions complete
- Long break after 4 pomodoros to prevent burnout

### 🛡️ **Intelligent Website Blocking**
Stop procrastination before it starts. Block distractions only when you need to focus.

- **Pattern Matching**: Block entire categories with keywords (e.g., "social", "news", "gaming")
- **Flexible Rules**: Block specific sites like `youtube.com` or `reddit.com` with one click
- **100+ Pre-Suggested Sites**: Quick-add popular time-wasters organized by category
- **Strict Mode**: Lock yourself in—prevent disabling the extension during work sessions
- **Whitelist Mode**: Block everything *except* your essential work tools
- **Smart Scheduling**: Auto-activate blocking during "Work Hours" (Mon-Fri, 9-5) or custom schedules

### 📊 **Motivating Analytics & Gamification**
See your progress. Stay motivated. Build momentum.

- **Daily Streaks**: Build consistency and watch your streak grow 🔥
- **Achievement Badges**: Unlock "Streak Warrior", "Focus Beast", and more
- **Visual Heatmaps**: See your productivity patterns at a glance
- **Focus Time Tracking**: Monitor total focused hours and completed pomodoros
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify your most productive days and times

### 🎨 **3 Immersive Themes**
Your workspace, your style. Choose a theme that helps you focus.

- **Modern Pro**: Sleek, professional dark mode for deep work
- **Zen Mode**: Calming earth tones and organic design to reduce stress
- **Cyber Focus**: High-contrast neon terminal aesthetic for coding sessions

Each theme includes matching notification sounds for a cohesive experience.

## 🔒 Privacy-First Design

**Your data belongs to you.** Period.

- ✅ **100% Local Storage** - All settings, rules, and analytics stay on your device
- ✅ **Zero Tracking** - We don't collect, analyze, or sell your browsing data
- ✅ **No Account Required** - Start using Focus Flow immediately, no sign-up needed
- ✅ **Open Permissions** - We only request what's necessary (see below)

### Why We Need Permissions

We believe in transparency. Here's exactly why Focus Flow needs each permission:

- **Storage** - Save your settings, blocking rules, and productivity stats locally
- **Alarms** - Accurately track timer duration and trigger notifications
- **Notifications** - Alert you when work sessions or breaks end
- **DeclarativeNetRequest** - Modern, privacy-preserving website blocking (blocks requests before they load)
- **Host Permissions (<all_urls>)** - Enforce your custom blocking rules on any website you choose
